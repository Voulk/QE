# Venomous Abyss Raid

Midnight Season 2 introduces a new raid - Venomous Abyss.

The following guides are designed around Heroic, with brief snippets at the bottom on Mythic mechanics.  The strategies within will work on Normal mode as well with reduced mechanics – it’s always good to practice good habits!

## Boss Guides

The Venomous Abyss is a new 8 boss raid. The raid opens on Normal, Heroic, and Mythic difficulties on March 18th.

## Raid Information
The raids include 4 tiers of item level, increasing its starting base level as you progress.
- **Tier 1 (Early Boss):** (Nek'zali) 305 IL loot from Heroic.
- **Tier 2 (Middle Bosses):** (Entombed Sentinels, The Lost Explorers) 308 IL loot from Heroic.
- **Tier 3 (Late Bosses):** (Vashnik, Sszorak, The Twin Fangs) 311 IL loot from Heroic.
- **Tier 4 (End Bosses):** (The Coiled Altar, Ula'tek) 315 IL loot from Heroic. 
Tier 4 bosses also drop additional crests at one track higher than their difficulty (so mythic crests from heroic).
